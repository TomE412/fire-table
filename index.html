CHANGES FROM YOUR BRIEF
Rails cross the slats, not parallel. Two 1200 rails running with the wood only meet the outer two slats — the other nine have nothing to bolt into. Turned 90°, every slat bolts to both rails.
Bin turned so its 600 runs with the slats. A 600 opening across the slats lands mid-slat at both edges (slat pitch is 110). Along the slats it only cuts 3 slats and leaves a clean 340 × 680 hole with 20 mm air gap to the 300 × 600 bin.
Bin hangs on its own steel. Lip rests on cradle bars ③ carried by bearers ②. Wood never touches the bin. Cut slat ends sit on trimmers ④.

WHY THE CENTRE PIN ALONE WON'T ADJUST HEIGHT
Your photo's table has both leg tops fixed — the pin only lets it fold. To change height, one leg top must move. So: frame A hinges to the rail at one end; frame B's top bar carries a shoe that slides along the other rail and drops into notches.
Notch distance from hinge (1000 leg, pin at mid-length):
750 table → 760
600 table → 866
450 table → 937
Frames must nest. A is 1000 outer width; B is 890 outer so it passes inside A. Pivot bolts go through the touching vertical legs, with a washer between.
Lock it. Add an R-clip pin through the shoe at each notch so a knock can't lift it out with a fire burning.

ASSEMBLY SEQUENCE
1. Tack ①⑤② on a flat bench. Diagonals equal ±2. Weld W1, W2.
2. Fit ④ flush, ③ 30 down with the bin sitting in. Weld W3, W4.
3. Weld hinge tabs W5 at X 150.
4. Weld frames A and B (W7, W8). Drill pivots, bolt B inside A.
5. Hang A on hinges, weld axle W9. Trial set 750 / 600 / 450, mark notches, cut ⑪, weld W6.
6. Grind sharp edges. Zinc primer + enamel. High-temp paint on ③ and the bin. Slats fitted last.
Top faces of ①②④⑤ must be flat — no weld bead proud where slats sit.

NOTES FOR THE LASER CUTTER
Cut on layer CUT only. Layer BEND_DO_NOT_CUT is the bend line — etch or mark, never cut through.
Slots already include 1 mm clearance (3 mm plate). Cut to drawn size, no extra offset.
Flat is drawn to inside dimensions. Bender to apply his bend deduction so each inside slant comes out at 291.5.
No press brake? Cut P1 as two halves split on the red line — adds one 594 mm seam weld at the bottom.
ASSEMBLY — section across the bin
1. Bend P1 on the red line to a 62° V.
2. Push P1's 4 end tabs through the slots in both P2 end plates.
3. Drop P3 on top: 6 tabs come up through its slots.
4. Clamp square, tack all 10 tabs from outside / on top.
5. Recommended: 25 mm stitch at each lower end corner (4) to stop the ends springing open when hot.
Bin: 300 wide · 600 long outside (594 inside) · 250 deep · lifts out of the table
